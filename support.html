Luma support

Luma is a media player and TV guide for Mac and Apple TV, using sources you choose. It does not supply television channels, movies, shows or provider accounts. Use only sources you are authorized to access.

Get started

Try Luma’s original offline sample without an account. On Mac, add a compatible Xtream account or M3U playlist in Accounts, or a WebDAV folder in Connections. On Apple TV, open Settings → Sources to add your source. Your source determines which titles, channels, schedules, artwork, audio tracks and subtitles are available. Luma does not provide a source account or media rights.

A channel or title will not play

Check that your source is available and that the address and account details you entered are current. Try another item from the same source. Some provider functions and media formats may not be compatible with Luma. If you use an HTTP source, review Luma’s connection warning before enabling it. Your provider is responsible for its service and content availability.

Purchases and subscriptions

Luma Premium has monthly and yearly auto-renewing options with the same feature level. On Mac, the free tier can play your connected source and may show a local Luma Premium message during Live TV; Premium removes that message and unlocks Kids and Favorites. On Apple TV, the free tier offers the offline sample and browsing; Premium unlocks playback from added sources, Kids and Favorites. Apple TV has no Luma ads. A purchase does not include channels, films, shows, media rights or a provider account. Check the in-app purchase sheet for the current price, period and renewal terms. Use Settings → Restore Purchases if an active subscription is missing. On Mac, Settings → Manage Subscription opens Apple’s subscription management; on Apple TV, follow the management path shown in Settings → Premium.

Privacy and removing a source

On Mac, remove a source from Accounts or Connections. On Apple TV, open Settings → Sources and remove it there. Read our privacy policy for details about local storage and provider requests.

Contact support

Email luma@dryg.nu. Include your Mac or Apple TV model, operating-system and Luma versions, what you expected and what happened. Please do not send passwords, full playlist or stream URLs, payment details, or private provider credentials.

For Apple billing or refund requests, use Apple’s purchase support.
