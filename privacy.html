Privacy policy

Luma is a media player and TV guide for Mac and Apple TV. It uses media sources you choose. This policy describes the information the apps handle, including optional Apple TV playback diagnostics, local Premium promotions on Mac, and purchases through Apple.

Information stored on your device

Luma saves source names, addresses and settings on the Mac or Apple TV where you enter them. Xtream and WebDAV credentials are stored in that device’s Keychain. Imported M3U playlist and stream URLs are stored in encrypted local app data with a key in Keychain. Luma may cache source-supplied catalog and guide details, artwork, viewing progress, favorites and browsing choices so the app remains responsive. Kids audio discovery may inspect media information from a chosen source after you enable or start that feature. Information you add on one device is not automatically copied to the other.

The original offline sample can be used without a provider login. Its media is generated and stored locally.

Connections to your chosen source

When you add a provider account, playlist or WebDAV folder, Luma contacts the server you choose to request catalogs, guides and playback. That server receives the connection information needed to serve those requests, which may include credentials or tokens in provider-specific request URLs. Your provider may also see your network address. Check your provider’s privacy policy for its handling of those requests.

Luma defaults to HTTPS and requires your explicit choice for an initial HTTP source. A provider may redirect media to HTTP or supply HTTP playlist segments, which are not encrypted in transit. Luma does not provide or verify rights to third-party content.

Advertising, purchases and analytics

Luma does not operate a cloud account or general usage analytics. The Mac app can display a static, locally bundled message about Luma Premium during free connected Live TV playback; this message makes no ad-network request. The Apple TV app does not display ads. No Google Ad Manager or other production ad network is active in these builds. Monthly and yearly Premium purchases, when offered in the app, are handled by Apple. Luma reads verified StoreKit transactions and subscription status to grant or restore access; Apple handles payment details under its own privacy policy. We will update this policy before enabling a third-party ad service or other new data collection.

Optional Apple TV playback diagnostics

In the Apple TV app, you can turn on Share playback diagnostics in Settings → About. It is off by default. When on, Luma sends a random identifier for each playback attempt, the app version and build, a broad content type (live channel, movie or episode), playback stages, elapsed time and a limited error code to codeandcrayon.com. This helps us investigate playback that stalls or fails on a physical Apple TV. The diagnostic event does not include your provider address, credentials, account identifier, stream URL, content title or raw player logs.

The server necessarily receives your network address to deliver the request, but the diagnostic event database does not store it. Diagnostic events are deleted after seven days. Turning the switch off stops new diagnostic uploads; existing events expire under the same schedule. This optional feature applies to Apple TV only and is not active in older builds that lack the switch.

Removing information

On Mac, remove a source in Accounts or Connections. On Apple TV, remove it in Settings → Sources. Luma removes the saved source and its credentials and clears associated local catalog, guide, artwork and account-owned activity where available. Some cached artwork may remain until cache expiry or app removal. You can also remove local app data by uninstalling the app; Apple TV and Mac store their data separately.

This website and contact

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. Visiting them sends ordinary website requests to GitHub, which handles them under its privacy statement. These pages contain no third-party analytics scripts or advertising.

For privacy questions or help removing local data, email luma@dryg.nu.
